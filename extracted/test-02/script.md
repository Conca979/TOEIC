## Part 1

**Directions:** For each question, you will listen to four short statements about a picture in your test book. These statements will not be printed and will only be spoken one time. Select the statement that best describes what is happening in the picture and mark the corresponding letter (A), (B), (C), or (D) on the answer sheet.

### Example
Look at the picture below. Now, listen to the four statements.
- **(A)** The man is looking at the monitor.
- **(B)** The man is sitting at the desk.
- **(C)** The man is typing a letter.
- **(D)** The man is holding a cup.

The statement that best describes the picture is **(B)**, "The man is sitting at the desk." So, you should mark letter **(B)** on the answer sheet. Now, Part 1 will begin.
